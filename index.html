      Snowfall, Frost & Ice Monitor

      Ground frost in the hill country, hail from thunderstorms and the freezing level, plus a snow watch for places abroad

      Sri Lanka frost & hail watch

      Loading…

      Snow has never been recorded in Sri Lanka. Ground frost forms on clear, calm nights when the air falls below about 4 °C, mainly at Nuwara Eliya, Ambewela and Horton Plains from December to March. It can damage vegetables and tea. Hail can fall from strong thunderstorms anywhere on the island.
